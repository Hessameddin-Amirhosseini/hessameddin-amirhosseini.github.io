VR + DIGITAL TWIN
Publication · CSME 2026

A Human-Centered UI/UX Design for Operator Training in a Manufacturing System via an Immersive Environment

A VR training environment built on Standard Operating Procedures and integrated with a digital twin of the SIF-400 smart manufacturing station, including its sensors, actuators, kinematics, and PLC I/O signals. An audiovisual guidance system teaches component function, and usability was evaluated with the System Usability Scale.

Proceedings of the Canadian Society for Mechanical Engineering. Presented at the CSME-CFDSC-CSR 2026 Congress, UBC, Vancouver.

How my work connects

My research links the physical production system, its virtual counterpart, and the operator who learns to run it.

Model

Build digital twins of manufacturing stations with Tecnomatix Process Simulate, Plant Simulation, and TIA Portal, linked to real PLC signals.

Immerse

Bring the twin into VR and XR so operators can explore and operate equipment safely before touching the real line.

Guide

Design human-centered, SOP-based guidance that explains each component and its role in the process.

Evaluate

Measure usability and learning so training outcomes can be assessed, not just delivered.
